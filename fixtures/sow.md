# Statement of Work — Meridian Jewellers Website

**Client:** Meridian Jewellers (Pune)
**Contractor:** independent web developer
**Date:** 28 July 2026
**Fee:** INR 85,000, fixed. 40% on signing, 60% on delivery.

## 1. Deliverables

The contractor will build a five-page static marketing website consisting of Home, Collections, About, Contact, and Store Locator pages.

The site will be responsive and render correctly on current mobile and desktop browsers.

A contact form will be included on the Contact page, delivering submissions by email to the store's registered address.

Basic on-page search engine optimisation is included: page titles, meta descriptions, and an XML sitemap.

The contractor will deploy the finished site to the client's existing hosting account.

## 2. Revisions

One round of revisions is included following delivery of the completed site. Revisions must be requested within fourteen days of delivery.

## 3. Explicitly excluded

Online payments, shopping cart, and any e-commerce functionality are excluded from this agreement. Content translation and multi-language versions of the site are excluded. Ongoing maintenance, hosting fees, and photography are excluded.

## 4. Timeline

Delivery within four weeks of signing. Delays caused by late delivery of client content extend the timeline accordingly.

## 5. Changes

Any work not described in Section 1 requires a written change order and separate fee agreement before the contractor begins that work.
