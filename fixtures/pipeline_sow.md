# Consulting Agreement — Northwind Freight Co.

This agreement is made 6 July 2026 between Northwind Freight Co. ("the Company") and the Contractor, an independent data engineer.

## Engagement

The Contractor will design and build an internal reporting pipeline that consolidates the Company's shipment records from its existing operational database into a warehouse table refreshed nightly, together with a set of internal dashboards for the operations team covering on-time delivery rate, average dwell time, and carrier cost per shipment.

The dashboards contemplated by this agreement are internal tools intended for employees of the Company. Anything intended for viewing by the Company's customers, shippers, or other external parties falls outside this engagement and would be quoted separately.

The Contractor will provide handover documentation sufficient for a competent engineer to operate and modify the pipeline, and will walk the operations team through the dashboards once before the engagement closes.

## Fees and hours

The Contractor bills at USD 95 per hour. The parties anticipate approximately 40 hours per month. Should the work in any month be trending materially above that figure, the Contractor will raise it with the Company before continuing rather than after the fact, and the parties will agree in writing how to proceed.

Invoices are issued monthly and payable within 21 days.

## Support

The Contractor will correct defects in their own work at no charge for 30 days following handover. Monitoring, paging, out-of-hours availability, and any form of on-call rotation are not part of this agreement; the Company understands that a pipeline failure at 2am will be looked at during the Contractor's next working day unless a separate support arrangement has been agreed.

## Data

The Company will provide read access to the operational database. The Contractor will not migrate, restructure, or otherwise alter the operational database itself, and any work touching production systems beyond reading from them requires the Company's written approval in advance.

## Changes

Work not described under Engagement above is outside this agreement. Either party may propose a change; it takes effect only once both have agreed it in writing, including its effect on hours and fees.

## Term

Either party may end this agreement with 14 days written notice. Work completed to that point is invoiced normally.
