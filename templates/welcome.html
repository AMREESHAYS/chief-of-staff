Welcome, {{ user.display_name }}.

Between you and Chief of Staff, from today.

Most tools open on an empty dashboard and leave you to work out what they are for. This one is easier to say plainly: from here on, no client asks you for something outside your contract without you being told which line it falls outside, and no promise you make goes quietly past its date.

What it will do for you

  1. Read your agreement

    Every clause becomes something the system can point at, quoted word for word. Nothing is paraphrased.

  2. Watch the thread

    Each message is judged against those clauses — in scope, outside it, a promise, or noise — with the line that decides it.

  3. Keep the ledger

    What you promised, when it was due, and what slipped. Including the ones where you only said “soon”.

  4. Draft, never send

    The reply is written and waits for you. Nothing leaves your account without you sending it yourself.

What it will never do

These are enforced in the code and covered by tests, not asked for politely in a prompt.

Never send on your behalf

Drafts are created and stop there. There is no send path in this software at all, and a test fails if one ever appears.

Never invent a number

No fee or percentage reaches a client unless that figure is already in your contract.

Never pick your date

When work is late, the update leaves the date blank. You cannot even approve it until you have chosen one.
